# Portfolio design guardrails

## Direction

Refined dark. The site should feel calm, senior, precise, and built by a full-stack engineer with product taste.

## Audience

Clients, hiring partners, and collaborators who need confidence that Pablo can shape, build, ship, and maintain production web products.

## Design rules

- Case studies are the primary proof. Screenshots, role, outcome, and shipped work should carry more weight than decoration.
- Keep the dark technical mood, but remove anything that feels busy, flashy, or over-styled.
- Use motion sparingly. It should clarify hierarchy or make interaction feel responsive, not perform for its own sake.
- Preserve bilingual content, current routes, Angular, Tailwind, and existing project assets.
- Prefer fewer, stronger surfaces over many glowing cards, rings, badges, and repeated labels.

## Anti-goal

Do not turn the portfolio into a generic agency landing page, a neon AI template, or a motion demo.
